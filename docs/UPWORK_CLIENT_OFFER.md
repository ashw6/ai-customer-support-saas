# Upwork Client Offer

## Best First-Client Service Title

I will build an AI customer support chatbot trained on your PDFs, FAQs, and help docs.

## Simple Client Pitch

I build custom AI support assistants that answer customer questions from your business documents, FAQs, product guides, or support policies. The system can include a chat UI, ticket creation, admin dashboard, document upload, lead capture, and optional email follow-up.

## Who This Is For

- SaaS founders who want to reduce repetitive support questions
- Agencies that need a support chatbot for a client
- Coaches, course creators, and service businesses with many FAQs
- E-commerce teams with product, shipping, return, and warranty policies
- Internal teams that need a private document Q&A assistant

## Starter Package

Deliver a focused MVP:

- Chatbot UI
- Document upload or document ingestion
- AI answers grounded in client-provided documents
- Source citations when available
- Basic admin instructions
- Local or hosted setup guidance

Suggested price for the first Upwork client: start modestly to get a review, then raise rates after one successful delivery.

## Standard Package

Add business workflow features:

- User login
- Ticket creation
- Conversation history
- Lead capture
- Admin dashboard
- Email notifications or follow-up

## Premium Package

Add production and handoff features:

- Deployment support
- Custom branding
- Database setup
- Analytics dashboard
- Error handling and logging
- Documentation and walkthrough video

## Proposal Template

Hi, I can build this for you.

I recently built a full-stack AI customer support SaaS with React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, document upload, RAG-based chat, ticket management, lead capture, and admin analytics.

For your project, I would first confirm the exact documents, FAQs, and support workflows you want the assistant to handle. Then I would build a focused version that can answer from your content, show citations where possible, and capture tickets or leads when the customer needs human follow-up.

My suggested first milestone:

- Review your documents and support flow
- Build the chatbot interface
- Connect document-based AI answers
- Add ticket or lead capture
- Deliver setup instructions and a walkthrough

I can keep the first version lean so you get something usable quickly, then we can improve it after testing with real questions.

## Discovery Questions

Ask these before accepting work:

- What documents, FAQs, or URLs should the assistant use?
- Do users need login, or should the chatbot be public?
- Should unresolved questions create tickets, leads, or emails?
- Do you need deployment, or only source code and setup instructions?
- Which AI provider or hosting environment do you prefer?
- Are there privacy, compliance, or data retention requirements?

## Delivery Checklist

- Confirm scope in writing.
- Get sample documents from the client.
- Build a small working version first.
- Test with 15 to 30 realistic questions.
- Add clear setup instructions.
- Record a short walkthrough video.
- Ask for feedback before adding extra features.

## Important Positioning

Do not sell this as a generic giant SaaS. Sell it as a business outcome:

- "Reduce repetitive support questions"
- "Answer from your company documents"
- "Capture leads from support conversations"
- "Turn FAQs and PDFs into a working chatbot"
- "Give your team a support dashboard"
